# Third-party data

Qastellan's daily definitions are built from these free sources. Their
licences apply to the definition files, not to the app's code.

| Source | What we use | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [ClamAV](https://www.clamav.net/) signature databases (Cisco Talos) | Android hash signatures (`Andr.*`) | GPL-2.0 |
| [MalwareBazaar](https://bazaar.abuse.ch/) by abuse.ch | Hashes and family names of Android samples | CC0 |
| [Exodus Privacy](https://exodus-privacy.eu.org/) tracker list | Code signatures of known tracker libraries, for the app privacy report | Published by Exodus Privacy through its public API; used under their terms |
| [MVT indicators](https://github.com/mvt-project/mvt-indicators) (Amnesty International, Echap and others) | Spyware package names and hashes | As listed by each indicator file, mostly CC BY / CC BY-SA |

Qastellan is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of these projects.
